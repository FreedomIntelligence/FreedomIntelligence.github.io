Our goal is not only to make large models run on China-developed AI accelerators, but to make them efficient, reliable, and scalable. The team has built an end-to-end technical chain connecting large-scale Ascend training, model and framework adaptation, inference acceleration, operator and kernel optimization, benchmark validation, real-world delivery, and open ecosystem development.

Large-Scale Training on Ascend

The team has first-hand experience training large language models on thousands of China-developed AI processors. In collaboration with Peng Cheng Laboratory and other institutions, the AraLLaMA/AceGPT-v1.5 program trained 7B and 13B Arabic models using 2,368 Huawei Ascend 910A processors, covering continual pre-training, progressive vocabulary expansion, and model alignment.

Full-Stack Compute Optimization

The capability has evolved from using China-developed compute to systematically optimizing it. Optimization spans the model, serving system, inference framework, operator, and kernel layers.

Model
Reduce model-side serving cost

Compress system prompts and manage or compress KV Cache to reduce memory pressure and repeated computation.

Runtime
Tune the inference path

Adapt and optimize vLLM, SGLang, FlashAttention, and FlashInfer-style components for target workloads and hardware stacks.

Kernel
Optimize below the framework layer

Profile bottlenecks and improve CANN operators, kernels, memory movement, graph execution, and hardware utilization.

Service
Validate production behavior

Measure latency, throughput, concurrency, utilization, reliability, security, and deployment cost—not compatibility alone.

NumericBridge and Software Library Repair at Scale

Through NumericBridge, we diagnose and repair numerical and compatibility issues in existing AI software libraries at scale, across inference, supervised fine-tuning, and reinforcement learning. The workflow connects native setup, precision comparison, diagnosis, repair, and verification on the original workload, preserving evidence as reusable community cases.

Repairs retain model, hardware, and framework versions and are checked for precision and performance on the original workload. GitHub · Project and cases

Benchmark Validation and Open Evaluation

AccelMark connects cross-accelerator results with shared workloads, runner versions, environment fingerprints, and accuracy records. AccelMark-Agent further evaluates whether agents deliver valid serving improvements across A100, RTX 4090, V100, and Ascend 910C, including 2,000 search rounds in the fully crossed A100 matrix. Ascend is included in portability evaluation, but the supplied manuscript reports no valid final delivery on that track.

New Research Papers and Full Author Lists

Titles and results follow the manuscripts supplied by the team; full author lists follow the supplied author order. These works are listed as manuscripts; consult the linked paper pages for current review status.

Research manuscript
AscendAgent: Evolving Context to Fit the Harness in CANN Kernel Optimization

Ruijie Zhang, Juhao Liang, Youxin Jiang, Zhihang Lin, Tian Ding, Benyou Wang

Evolves reusable optimization knowledge while keeping model weights, the harness, and the evaluator fixed. With Kimi K3 and 40 candidate compilations per task, mean best speedups are 1.40× on CANN-Bench and 1.75× on TritonBench-G, versus 1.21× and 1.36× for the same harness with frozen initial knowledge.

Paper · GitHub (KernelBridge)
Research manuscript
AccelMark-Agent: Benchmarking Inference-Optimization Agents Across Accelerators and Workloads

Juhao Liang, Youxin Jiang, Zhiyuan Zhang, Ruijie Zhang, Zhihang Lin, Yuxin Fu, Shiqi Zhang, Siyu Li, Xiaolong Liu, Prayag Tiwari, Minchen Yu, Li Zeng, Zizhong Chen, Benyou Wang

Of 50 A100 cells, 26 delivered a changed artifact over 2% faster than baseline; no Ascend condition produced a valid final delivery.

OS-Native Agents: Autonomous Task Management in the Dynamic Mobile World

Qiming Zhu, Shunian Chen, Wentao Ge, Zhengyang Tang, Xiaoguang Li, Pinjia He, Benyou Wang

Integrates persistent task management with native OS state, capabilities, events, and permission checks. On Android, DynamicWorld contains 88 tasks across 16 scenarios. The supplied manuscript reports 78.4% task success with GPT-5.4 over three runs, versus 47.0% for MobileClaw, with 51.1% fewer model calls.

全栈算力优化

团队的能力已从使用国产算力，发展为系统性地优化国产算力。优化覆盖模型、推理服务系统、推理框架、算子和 Kernel 各个层次。

模型
降低模型侧的推理成本

压缩系统提示词，管理或压缩 KV 缓存，减轻显存压力，减少重复计算。

运行时
调优推理链路

针对目标负载和硬件栈，适配并优化 vLLM、SGLang、FlashAttention、FlashInfer 等组件。

Kernel
深入框架之下优化

分析性能瓶颈，改进 CANN 算子、Kernel、数据搬运、图执行和硬件利用率。

服务
验证生产环境表现

测量延迟、吞吐量、并发、利用率、可靠性、安全性和部署成本，而不只是检查能否兼容。

NumericBridge 规模化软件库修复

AscendAgent: Evolving Context to Fit the Harness in CANN Kernel Optimization

Ruijie Zhang, Juhao Liang, Youxin Jiang, Zhihang Lin, Tian Ding, Benyou Wang

在固定优化执行框架下演化可复用知识，通过任务内程序图搜索和任务间工作流回放积累算子优化经验。实验涵盖昇腾 NPU 与 NVIDIA GPU、4 个算子基准和 5 个语言模型，每项任务预算为 40 次候选编译。采用 Kimi K3 时，CANN-Bench 和 TritonBench-G 的平均最佳加速比分别为 1.40 倍和 1.75 倍，固定初始知识的同框架基线分别为 1.21 倍和 1.36 倍。
