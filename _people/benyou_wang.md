  <section class="benyou-section" id="research">
    <header class="benyou-section-heading">
      <p>01 · RESEARCH AGENDA</p>
      <h2><span data-lang="en">Intelligence that learns from the world</span><span data-lang="zh">从环境反馈中持续进化</span></h2>
      <div>
        <span data-lang="en">Intelligence should not stop improving after a single training run. We build environments where agents act, expose failures, receive feedback from verifiers, people and the world, and turn that evidence into better data, tools, policies and models.</span>
        <span data-lang="zh">智能不应止步于一次性训练。我们构建可重复、可扩展的环境，让智能体完成任务、暴露失败，获取来自验证器、人类和真实世界的反馈，再把这些证据转化为更好的数据、工具、策略与模型。</span>
      </div>
    </header>

    <div class="benyou-rsi-panel">
      <img src="{{ '/assets/img/benyou-rsi-hero.webp' | relative_url }}" alt="Abstract visualization of recursive learning loops" loading="lazy">
      <div class="benyou-rsi-copy">
        <p class="benyou-kicker">RECURSIVE SELF-IMPROVEMENT</p>
        <h3><span data-lang="en">Synthetic worlds for iteration.<br>Real worlds for proof.</span><span data-lang="zh">在合成世界中高效迭代，<br>在真实世界中验证价值。</span></h3>
        <p>
          <span data-lang="en">Synthetic environments create controlled, low-cost spaces for learning. Healthcare, finance, education, operations research and robotics then test robustness, generalization and practical value. The aim is not unconstrained self-modification, but reliable capability growth under explicit metrics, safety boundaries and human oversight.</span>
          <span data-lang="zh">合成环境提供低成本、可控的迭代空间；医疗、金融、教育、运筹优化与机器人等真实场景检验模型的鲁棒性、泛化能力和实际价值。我们追求的不是无约束的自我修改，而是在明确评价指标、安全边界和人类监督下，实现稳定、可复现的能力增长。</span>
        </p>
        <a href="https://openreview.net/forum?id=TA6azZKWJq" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><span data-lang="en">Read: Self-Evolving Critique Abilities →</span><span data-lang="zh">阅读：大模型的自进化批评能力 →</span></a>
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="benyou-loop" aria-label="Recursive self-improvement loop">
      <article><b>01</b><h3><span data-lang="en">Build environments</span><span data-lang="zh">构建环境</span></h3><p><span data-lang="en">Turn open-ended tasks into reproducible worlds, simulators and evaluation settings.</span><span data-lang="zh">把开放任务转化为可复现的世界、模拟器与评测场景。</span></p></article>
      <article><b>02</b><h3><span data-lang="en">Act and test</span><span data-lang="zh">行动与测试</span></h3><p><span data-lang="en">Let agents interact with tools, people and systems, then surface concrete failure modes.</span><span data-lang="zh">让智能体与工具、人和系统交互，暴露具体、可定位的失败模式。</span></p></article>
      <article><b>03</b><h3><span data-lang="en">Capture feedback</span><span data-lang="zh">收集反馈</span></h3><p><span data-lang="en">Combine verifiers, human judgment and environmental outcomes into learning signals.</span><span data-lang="zh">把验证器、人类判断与环境结果转化为可信学习信号。</span></p></article>
      <article><b>04</b><h3><span data-lang="en">Improve recursively</span><span data-lang="zh">递归改进</span></h3><p><span data-lang="en">Update data, memory, tools, policies and models—then return to a harder environment.</span><span data-lang="zh">更新数据、记忆、工具、策略和模型，再进入更具挑战的环境。</span></p></article>
    </div>
