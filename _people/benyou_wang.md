      <p class="benyou-section-label">Research vision and leadership</p>
      <h2><span data-lang="zh">从基础模型到真实世界智能</span><span data-lang="en">From foundation models to real-world intelligence</span></h2>
    </header>
    <p><span data-lang="zh">围绕“如何让人工智能持续学习，并在真实世界创造价值”这一长期问题，团队以基础模型为底座、以持续学习的智能体为研究主线、以高价值应用场景为牵引，形成相互支撑的研究布局。</span><span data-lang="en">The group's long-term agenda asks how AI can keep learning and create value in the real world. It connects foundation models, continuously learning agents, and high-value applications as mutually reinforcing research directions.</span></p>
    <ul class="benyou-research-agenda">
      <li><strong><span data-lang="zh">基础模型与训练方法。</span><span data-lang="en">Foundation models and training.</span></strong> <span data-lang="zh">贯通预训练、后训练与评测，在多语言、医疗和多模态方向积累大规模训练与开源实践，把方法创新沉淀为可复用的模型与工具。</span><span data-lang="en">Connect pre-training, post-training, and evaluation across multilingual, medical, and multimodal AI, turning methodological advances and large-scale training experience into reusable models and tools.</span></li>
      <li><strong><span data-lang="zh">持续学习的智能体。</span><span data-lang="en">Continuously learning agents.</span></strong> <span data-lang="zh">研究推理、工具使用、环境反馈与递归自提升，建设可重复的任务环境和评测体系，让能力提升可以被验证、积累和迭代。</span><span data-lang="en">Study reasoning, tool use, environmental feedback, and recursive self-improvement, with reproducible task environments and evaluations that make progress measurable and cumulative.</span></li>
      <li><strong><span data-lang="zh">真实场景与技术转化。</span><span data-lang="en">Real-world applications and translation.</span></strong> <span data-lang="zh">连接医疗、运筹优化、金融、教育与机器人等场景，将应用需求转化为研究问题，并通过系统部署与产业合作检验研究价值。</span><span data-lang="en">Turn needs in healthcare, optimization, finance, education, and robotics into research questions, and assess their value through deployed systems and industry collaboration.</span></li>
    </ul>
    <p><span data-lang="zh">这一布局强调科研方向判断、跨学科团队组织和从模型到系统的执行能力：将长期研究目标分解为可验证的阶段成果，通过开放模型、数据、评测与合作网络持续积累团队能力。</span><span data-lang="en">This agenda brings together research direction, interdisciplinary team building, and execution from models to systems: translating long-term goals into verifiable milestones and building lasting capabilities through open models, data, evaluation, and collaboration.</span></p>
